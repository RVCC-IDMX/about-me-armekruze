Biography

I am currently a dedicated student at Raritan Valley Community College, where I am studying to become a Nurse. My life is driven by the mission to help others and serve my community. I am very proud of the practical experience I've gained through four years as a Physical Therapy Aide, a job I continue to work. This experience has deepened my commitment to patient care and my philosophy of respect, which extends to my personal life, especially in helping to care for my in-laws and showing respect to my elders. Right now, my biggest goal is finishing school so I can become a full-time nursing and broaden my ability to assist people in need. When I step away from my studies, I find balance and joy in nature. I love hiking in the tri-state area, spending time with my beloved dog, Suzie, and fixing up my home through gardening and caring for my house plants. I also enjoy catching a good movie or going to the theater. My ultimate goal is to live a life built on compassion, service, and nurturing those around me.

Hobbies

  • Gardening

    • My passion for horticulture flourishes both outdoors and indoors. My outdoor garden is a vibrant landscape, intentionally cultivated with a variety of flowers, shrubs, and perennials that provide continuous color and interest throughout the seasons. This natural space is complemented by my indoor plant collection featuring plants like Bamboo, various hanging plants, and several types of ferns. My ferns are, by far, the most rewarding plants to care for, as I get much satisfaction from nurturing them to their maximum and they grow to impressive size, whether they are growing outside or thriving within the home. A crucial piece of advice I've learned for any gardener is to master the art of watering. Plants have highly specific hydration needs; some require only a light spritz, delicate species like Orchids benefit from the slow release of an ice cube, and others demand the soil be completely soaked. Understanding the unique requirements of each plant is the key to healthy, flourishing greenery.

  • Movies

    • My movie preferences lean toward thrillers, but not horror based thrillers, and Action films. I am drawn to cinema that features compelling characters and heart-pounding action. I frequently follow the work of specific leading actors known for their dynamic roles and commitment to the action genre, including Keanu Reeves, Liam Neeson, Jason Statham, Dwayne Johnson, Jason Momoa, and Matt Damon. While I don't follow a specific director or franchise exclusively, the James Bond series is a notable exception that I can rewatch repeatedly. The appeal of the Bond films lies in the powerful Hero's Theme, the depiction of a courageous, resourceful protagonist who embodies justice and never fails to deliver a captivating adventure.

  • Pet Care

    • The focus of my pet care world is my sweet, twelve year old female Jack Russell Terrier, Suzie. Caring for a senior dog requires prioritizing her comfort, maintaining a consistent routine, and ensuring she receives plenty of quality time and affection. Suzie is the perfect example of a loyal and loving companion. The most rewarding and joyful aspect of being her owner is whenever I enter she is so happy to see me. She demonstrates her affection by following me everywhere. She is a constant, comforting shadow not because she expects a treat or a meal, but simply because she wants to be near me. Her consistent following shows our connection between a owner and their pet.

Alice in Wonder land

Alice was beginning to get very tired of sitting by her sister on the bank, and of having nothing to do: once or twice she had peeped into the book her sister was reading, but it had no pictures or conversations in it, 'and what is the use of a book,' thought Alice 'without pictures or conversation?'

So she was considering in her own mind (as well as she could, for the hot day made her feel very sleepy and stupid), whether the pleasure of making a daisy-chain would be worth the trouble of getting up and picking the daisies, when suddenly a White Rabbit with pink eyes ran close by her.

The rabbit-hole went straight on like a tunnel for some way, and then dipped suddenly down, so suddenly that Alice had not a moment to think about stopping herself before she found herself falling down a very deep well. Either the well was very deep, or she fell very slowly, for she had plenty of time as she went down to look about her and to wonder what was going to happen next.
